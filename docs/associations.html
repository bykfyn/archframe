The real trade and craft associations whose public member data seeds this directory. Archframe doesn't replace an association's own membership criteria — it re-presents their members with real photos, search, and a shared profile that can span more than one association.

Föreningen Skråhantverkarna

A Swedish association of traditional craftspeople, formed in 1983 to preserve trades and skills that might otherwise disappear - bookbinders, gilders, violin-makers, silversmiths and other heritage crafts, alongside several trades that overlap furniture and interior work.
